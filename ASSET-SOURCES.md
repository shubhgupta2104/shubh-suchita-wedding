## TL;DR

- The couple supplied their logo and music recording.
- The invitation uses the five supplied illustrations approved on 2 October, including the refined Phere setting, closer fire and full original Sangeet portrait.

- Font and sample-video licenses are listed below. This repository does not grant reuse rights to the logo or music.

## Why this matters

The artwork evokes the wedding without presenting representative figures as portraits or illustrations as photographs.

## Impact

No third-party scripts, trackers, font requests or media requests are needed to render the invitation. The user-selected recording starts at 25 seconds when a guest opens or skips the entrance; a music button remains available to pause it. The audio is not a newly generated instrumental or a vocal-isolated version. Loading the map contacts Google.

No official venue photography or Vogue/celebrity imagery is used. The user-supplied logo and recording retain their separate rights.

The Pexels License was read directly on 1 October 2026.
