## TL;DR

- The couple supplied their logo and music recording.

## Why this matters

The artwork evokes the wedding without presenting representative figures as portraits or illustrations as photographs.

## Impact

No third-party scripts, trackers, font requests or media requests are needed to render the invitation. The user-selected recording starts at 12 seconds when a guest opens or skips the entrance; a music button remains available to pause it. The audio is not a newly generated instrumental or a vocal-isolated version. Loading the map contacts Google.

## Risks and open questions

The figures, outfits and wedding decor are illustrative. Actual event locations within the property are not specified. No official venue photography is copied into this site.
